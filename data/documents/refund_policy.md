# Refund Policy and Processing Procedure

**Document owner:** Billing Operations
**Version:** 3.2
**Last reviewed:** 2026-01-15
**Applies to:** All customer-facing support and billing staff

## Purpose

This document defines how refunds are evaluated, approved and processed. It is the
authoritative internal reference for refund handling. Where this document conflicts
with a statement made by an agent or a third-party article, this document governs
for company policy questions.

## Refund Eligibility Windows

Refund eligibility depends on the plan type and the reason for the request.

| Plan | Standard refund window | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly subscription | 14 days from the charge date | Pro-rated after 7 days |
| Annual subscription | 30 days from the charge date | Full refund only within 30 days |
| One-time purchase | 14 days from purchase | Must be unused or under 10% consumed |
| Enterprise contract | Per contract terms | Escalate to the account manager |

Requests received outside these windows are declined by default, but may be approved
as a goodwill exception under the criteria in the Goodwill Exceptions section.

## Duplicate and Erroneous Charges

Duplicate charges are **not** subject to the standard refund window. A verified
duplicate charge is always refunded in full, regardless of how much time has passed.

To verify a duplicate charge:

1. Retrieve the customer's billing history for the disputed period.
2. Confirm two or more charges of the same amount to the same payment method within
   a 24-hour window.
3. Confirm that only one corresponding subscription or purchase record exists.
4. If all three conditions hold, the charge is a verified duplicate.

Verified duplicate charges are processed as a priority refund with a target of one
business day. They do not require the approval of a billing manager, but they do
require the standard refund approval step described below.

## Refund Processing Procedure

All refunds follow the same six steps.

1. **Verify the customer identity.** Confirm the account email and the last four
   digits of the payment method before discussing any charge detail.
2. **Retrieve the charge record.** Use the billing system to pull the original
   transaction, including the payment processor reference.
3. **Determine eligibility.** Apply the eligibility windows above, or the duplicate
   charge rule if applicable.
4. **Obtain approval.** Every refund requires explicit approval before it is issued.
   Approval authority is defined in the Approval Authority section. No refund may be
   issued automatically by an automated system or agent without a recorded human
   approval.
5. **Issue the refund.** Refunds are returned to the original payment method. We do
   not issue refunds to a different card, account or payee.
6. **Record the outcome.** Add a comment to the ticket recording the amount, the
   approver and the processor reference. Close the ticket only after the refund is
   confirmed as submitted.

## Approval Authority

| Refund amount | Approver |
| --- | --- |
| Up to 50 USD | Any support agent |
| 50.01 to 500 USD | Support team lead |
| 500.01 to 5,000 USD | Billing manager |
| Above 5,000 USD | Finance director and billing manager |

Enterprise and platinum-tier accounts require account manager sign-off at any amount.

## Refund Timelines

Once a refund is submitted, the funds return on the payment processor's schedule.

- Credit and debit cards: 5 to 10 business days
- UPI and net banking (India): 3 to 7 business days
- PayPal: 3 to 5 business days
- Wire transfer: 7 to 14 business days

Agents must communicate these as processor timelines, not as company commitments.
The company controls only the submission date.

## Goodwill Exceptions

A refund outside the standard window may be approved as a goodwill exception when at
least one of the following applies:

- A documented service outage affected the customer during the billing period.
- The customer was charged after a cancellation request was submitted and recorded.
- A support error caused or prolonged the issue.
- The account is enterprise or platinum tier and the account manager requests it.

Goodwill exceptions always require team lead approval or above, regardless of amount.

## Non-Refundable Items

The following are not refundable under any circumstances:

- Usage-based overage charges that have already been consumed.
- Third-party marketplace fees passed through at cost.
- Custom professional-services engagements after work has commenced.

## Escalation

Escalate to the billing manager when:

- The customer disputes the outcome after a decision has been communicated.
- The refund amount exceeds the agent's approval authority.
- A chargeback has already been filed with the card issuer.
- The request involves a suspected fraudulent transaction. In this case also raise a
  security escalation.
