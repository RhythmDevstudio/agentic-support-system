BILLING FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Document owner: Billing Operations
Version: 1.8
Last reviewed: 2026-01-28

Q: Why was I charged twice this month?

A: The most common cause is a retry after an initial authorization failure. The
first charge usually appears as a pending authorization and drops off within 3 to 5
business days without ever settling. If both charges have settled, it is a duplicate
charge and is refunded in full regardless of the standard refund window. Refer to the
Refund Policy document for the duplicate charge verification steps.

Q: When will my refund appear?

A: The company controls only the submission date. Once submitted, the funds return on
the payment processor's schedule: 5 to 10 business days for cards, 3 to 7 business
days for UPI and net banking, 3 to 5 business days for PayPal. Never promise a date
the processor controls.

Q: Can I get a refund to a different card?

A: No. Refunds are always returned to the original payment method. If that card is
closed, the customer must contact their issuing bank, which is obliged to forward the
funds to the replacement account.

Q: What is the difference between a cancellation and a refund?

A: Cancellation stops future billing. It does not by itself return money already
charged. A customer who wants both must be handled as two actions: a cancellation
processed by the Retention queue and a refund evaluated under the Refund Policy.

Q: Why did my subscription renew after I cancelled?

A: Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period unless the
customer explicitly requests immediate termination. If a charge occurred after a
recorded cancellation request, that is a billing error and qualifies for a goodwill
refund exception.

Q: The customer is disputing a charge with their bank. What do I do?

A: Once a chargeback is filed, stop processing the refund and escalate to the billing
manager. Issuing a refund on a charge that is already under dispute results in a
double credit that must then be recovered from the customer.

Q: A customer says their payment failed but money left their account.

A: This is an authorization hold, not a settled charge. Holds release automatically,
typically within 3 to 5 business days. Confirm in the billing system that no
settlement record exists before telling the customer it will release.

Q: What currencies do we bill in?

A: USD, EUR, GBP and INR. The billing currency is fixed at signup and cannot be
changed on an existing subscription. Changing currency requires cancelling and
re-subscribing, which the Retention queue handles.

Q: Are taxes refundable?

A: Tax collected is refunded proportionally with the refunded amount. Tax already
remitted to an authority in a closed period is still refunded to the customer; the
recovery is handled internally by Finance and is not the customer's concern.
